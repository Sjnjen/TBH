About

Since 2024.

The Original Elevation: street art, loud ideas, and a brand built by one boy, for everyone.

Identity

The Boy Himself (TBH) is an independent streetwear label started in 2024. Every design is its own statement, released in limited runs.

Philosophy

Be original, aim higher. Each tee carries our tagline, The Original Elevation, and is made to be worn loud.

Inspiration

Graffiti walls, drips and splatter, tattoo flash, and mascot art, set against a clean black-and-white canvas.

Shipping & returns.
